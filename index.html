Teacher notes, photograph credits, and privacy

Content aligned to BASIS History 6 Unit 1: Early Human Societies, including all 23 key vocabulary entries. Connections are limited to pre-comprehensive topics (especially Unit 2). Scenarios and resource meters are teaching simulations, not reconstructed historical events or measurements. Prehistoric art and the oldest stone tools illustrate different periods and should not be treated as the same time.

Real photos hosted on Wikimedia Commons: Serengeti plains — רנדום, CC BY-SA 3.0; Olduvai Gorge — Clem23, CC BY-SA 3.0; Lascaux cave painting — Prof saxx, CC BY-SA 3.0; Çatalhöyük excavation — Omar Hoftun, CC BY-SA 3.0; Olduvai chopper — BabelStone, CC BY-SA 3.0; Ziggurat of Ur — wikiwikiyarou / Ensahequ, public domain. Images are linked rather than bundled and require internet access.

No login, grades, analytics, or submissions. Checkpoint progress is saved on the current browser only, if local storage is available. Learning games use simplified historical models; no single migration route or social structure describes all early humans.
